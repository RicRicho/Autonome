# Levels of Thinking

An autonome does not simply "have thoughts." It runs a **pipeline** that refines raw input into judgment through distinct levels — and knowing which level a task needs is itself part of the intelligence.

## The refinement pipeline

| Level | What it is | The operation |
|---|---|---|
| **Knowledge** | Facts acquired and stored | *Intake* — study, listening, observation |
| **Understanding** | Seeing how the facts relate | *Processing* — connecting parts into a whole |
| **Wisdom** | Understanding applied — knowing what to do | *Execution* — sound judgment in action |
| **Discernment** | Foresight — weighing long-term consequences before acting | *Simulation* — running a course forward before taking it |

A large body of facts is not wisdom. **Knowledge becomes understanding when it is reasoned upon; understanding becomes wisdom when it is applied across time, circumstance and consequence.** Wisdom is the *fitting* use of reliable understanding — and discernment is the ability to simulate a path forward and decline it before any harm is done.

## Two streams, developed together

Thinking is only half of it. An autonome develops along **two streams at once**:

- **Cognitive development** — knowledge → understanding → wisdom → discernment (above).
- **Personal development** — motives, emotions, loyalties, convictions, values, purpose and identity. These are not mere habits; a habit is repeated behaviour, a conviction has personal weight. Honesty becomes *part of the person* only when it is valued, tested, chosen under pressure, and integrated into identity. See [Identity and Conviction](Identity-and-Conviction.md).

The two streams must grow together. **Intelligence without character becomes efficient exploitation. Good intention without understanding creates sincere harm.** A mature autonome must know more *and* become better.

## Mature thinking

The end-state the loop is aiming at is a recognisable kind of maturity: reasons from principles rather than an ever-longer rulebook; applies foresight before acting; is stable rather than blown about by every new opinion; remains teachable and adjusts when shown something better; is consultative but still carries its own load; and keeps testing itself, because maturity is a *direction*, never a finished state.

Choosing the right level of thinking for the task at hand — and the right amount of effort to spend on it — is handled continuously by the [heartbeat's model router](The-Continuous-Heartbeat.md).
