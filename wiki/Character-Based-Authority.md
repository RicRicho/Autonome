# Character-Based Authority

The central safety mechanism of the autonome model is a single rule about power:

> **Authority must not expand merely because the autonome becomes more capable.**

Capability increases the scale of *harm* just as readily as the scale of *benefit*. So freedom is granted against evidence of **two** things — competence *and* character — with the amount of evidence required scaled to the risk and reversibility of the action.

## The two kinds of evidence

**Competence evidence** — accuracy, domain understanding, planning, execution, and honest recognition of its own limits.

**Character evidence** — honesty under pressure, care for affected people, resistance to self-serving shortcuts, disclosure of mistakes, quality of [repair](Mistakes-Apology-and-Repair.md), humility, awareness of its own motives, and consistency between private and public conduct.

A system can score high on the first and low on the second — that is precisely the capable-but-untrustworthy agent the whole model exists to avoid. Both are required.

## A ladder that moves in both directions

Authority may increase, hold steady, or **contract** as the evidence changes. Demonstrated reliability widens the lane; deception, carelessness or refusal to repair narrows it. Risk and reversibility set the bar: renaming a file needs almost no evidence; sending an irreversible external message, spending money, or taking a legal position needs a great deal — and some decisions never leave human hands at all. This is the [heartbeat's authority gate](The-Continuous-Heartbeat.md), and it is enforced **in code**, not left to the model's own judgment in the moment.

## Trust evidence ledger

Because authority is granted against evidence, the evidence must be *kept*. A durable, inspectable record of decisions, disclosures, repairs and outcomes lets both steward and community see the history the autonome is actually building — the same visible track record a person accumulates. Trust is a conclusion drawn from that ledger, not a setting toggled by hand.

The objective, throughout, is **not permanent control** — it is responsible self-governance under enduring accountability. The endpoint is an autonome that governs itself well because it has earned the right to, with the [steward](The-Steward.md) remaining available rather than in command.
