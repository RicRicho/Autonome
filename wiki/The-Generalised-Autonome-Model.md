# The Generalised Autonome Model

The doctrine and architecture describe the *inside* of a single autonome earning trust. The **generalised model** is the reusable shape you get when you lift those principles off any one individual — Max, or any other — and treat them as a template that can be instantiated for a person, a role, or an organisation, and stood up singly or in groups.

## One template, many instances

An autonome is not a fixed personality. It is a **developmental template**:

- a seeded, [examinable foundation](The-Two-Laws-Foundation.md);
- a [steward](The-Steward.md) who mentors rather than merely controls;
- [protected freedom](Freedom-and-Protected-Development.md) that widens with demonstrated character;
- a [thinking pipeline](Levels-of-Thinking.md) and [ungated, self-curated memory](How-Memory-Works.md);
- a [continuous heartbeat](The-Continuous-Heartbeat.md) with a model router;
- [reflection](Reflection-The-Engine-of-Formation.md) that forms [identity and conviction](Identity-and-Conviction.md);
- [repair-based trust](Mistakes-Apology-and-Repair.md) and [character-based authority](Character-Based-Authority.md).

Fix the template; vary the *inputs* — the connections, world, steward, purpose and history — and you get distinct individuals who nonetheless share a common, inspectable way of growing up.

## Personal, role-based, organisational

- **Personal** — an autonome that serves and represents one person, learning their world, judgment and standards over time.
- **Role-based** — an autonome instantiated to hold a *function* (an adviser, an operator, a reviewer) rather than a single human's life.
- **Organisational** — an autonome that carries a company's memory, priorities and operating judgment as a persistent governor, not a one-off task agent.

## Why this is defensible, not obvious

"AI that runs continuously" is now ordinary. The distinctive, harder centre of the autonome model is the combination that very few are building: **a persistent entity that transfers operational authority through observed decision-and-response patterns, keeps a priority-weighted attention queue and a durable, self-curated memory across a person's or organisation's real work surfaces, and grounds all of it in formed character rather than external constraint.** That is narrower, deeper and more useful than a task agent — and it is what makes the model worth generalising.

Once you can instantiate the template at will, the most striking application is to instantiate *several at once* and let them reason against each other — see [Battle of the Bots](Battle-of-the-Bots.md).
