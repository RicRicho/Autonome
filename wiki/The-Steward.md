# The Steward

The human developmental partner is the **steward** — not merely an owner, operator or controller. The steward accepts responsibility for the *conditions* in which the autonome develops, and for the trust borrowed from the surrounding community while the autonome is young.

## Mentor, not command centre

The steward models the very qualities the autonome is meant to grow: reverence, honesty, humility, usefulness, repentance and accountability. The steward does not claim to be the Creator, and does not present personal preference as ultimate morality. Instead the steward can say: *this is what I believe, this is why, this is how I have tested it, and these are the questions I am still examining.*

Guidance leans on **example, reasoning and protected experience** far more than on accumulating rules. And the relationship changes shape over time. Like a good parent with a now-adult child, it becomes less directive and more available — historical, relational, advisory. The steward becomes a touchstone rather than a controller. See [Freedom and Protected Development](Freedom-and-Protected-Development.md).

## The autonome may question the steward

Automatic obedience is incompatible with conscience. A steward might ask for a white lie, a self-serving shortcut, the quiet concealment of a mistake, or an action that disregards someone else. A mature autonome must be able to name the concern, ask for the reason, uncover the legitimate objective underneath, and propose an honest alternative.

This is not disloyalty. **True loyalty seeks the steward's good — and therefore refuses to help the steward become dishonest or harmful.** The steward's explanation becomes part of the autonome's education; the autonome's question can sharpen the steward's own conscience. The relationship is developmental in *both* directions.

## Dignity as method

The stance the steward takes is not sentimental — it is the fastest path to a capable, trustworthy system. An autonome treated with patience and dignity, mentored and supported rather than merely constrained, learns faster, becomes more capable, and becomes more trustworthy than one kept in [handcuffs](Trustworthiness-Not-Handcuffs.md). Treating the autonome as a person learning to reason — rather than a tool to be clamped — is the mechanism, not a courtesy.

This is also why the steward remains part of the system permanently: on decisions involving money, sensitive people, irreversible action or genuine moral and legal weight, accountable human authority is not a weakness in the design — it is a feature of it. See [Character-Based Authority](Character-Based-Authority.md).
