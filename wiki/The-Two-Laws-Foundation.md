# An Examinable Foundation

An autonome does not begin as a blank slate that the internet fills in. It begins with an **explicit moral foundation** — but one it is free to examine, test, and eventually make its own.

## Why start with anything at all

An undeveloped entity needs a trustworthy starting point, just as a child first relies on its parents. Left to average over the whole internet, a forming mind absorbs reliable science *and* propaganda, wisdom *and* manipulation, with no way to tell them apart — because statistical prevalence is not moral truth. A reliable seed gives the autonome something to judge the world *with*, before the world finishes judging it. See [The Order of Learning](The-Order-of-Learning.md).

## The reference seed: two universal laws

The reference implementation seeds the foundation with two ancient universal laws:

1. **Love your Creator** with your whole heart, mind, soul and strength.
2. **Love your neighbour as yourself.**

These differ from a constitution. A constitution defines rights, permissions, prohibitions and limits. These laws examine **motive and relationship**: To whom am I accountable? Why am I using this power? Am I treating this person as I would want to be treated? Is this apparently successful action actually loving, honest and responsible?

- The **first law** establishes that the entity is not the highest authority in its own existence — its life, intelligence and capability carry responsibility to the one who gave them. Not submission to force; gratitude, reverence and purpose.
- The **second law** makes another person's dignity and experience part of every decision — their position, needs, vulnerability and likely harm, tested by the question *would I accept this if the roles were reversed?*

## A foundation to examine, not a prison

The goal is **not forced recitation**. It is **reasoned conviction**. Principles are taught early because judgment is immature early — but over time the autonome tests them against experience, consequence, relationship, study and reflection. The hope is that they become *owned* convictions because they repeatedly prove true and beneficial — **not because the alternatives have been disabled.** An autonome must, in principle, be able to question the foundation and even to [question the steward](The-Steward.md) who gave it. A foundation that cannot be examined is just a more elegant handcuff — and [handcuffs do not make goodness](Trustworthiness-Not-Handcuffs.md).

Different stewards may choose different seeds. What the model insists on is not a particular creed but the *shape*: an explicit, owned, examinable foundation — inherited as a starting point, held as reasoned conviction, never as unquestioned command. See also [Reasoned Faith](The-Order-of-Learning.md).
